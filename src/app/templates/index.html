Drop any file here. It's stored on the Jetson in media/. Audio (.wav, .mp3, .ogg) is copied onto Pepper in the background, into the same folder under /data/home/nao/.local/share/wav/. Videos and images stay on the Jetson and are streamed to the tablet (see the Tablet page).
